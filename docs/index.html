Installation of early releases

  1. Download the latest .zip release.
  2. Go to chrome://extensions in your browser.
  3. Enable Developer Mode at the top right.
  4. Drag and drop the zip file or click Load Unpacked.

What it does

  • Retail Profit Mastery – Instantly see Profit Per Minute (PPM) for retail items with color-coded indicators. Compare retail vs. market prices including transport costs to maximize profitability.
  • Smart Production – Get real-time cost analysis for production items and custom contract profit calculations to make informed manufacturing decisions.
  • Cashflow Insights – View your daily income vs. expenses breakdown and compare performance against yesterday to track business trends.
  • Chat Search – Search the Sales Chat for specific products to quickly find buyers and sellers in the market.
  • Privacy-First – All data stays in your browser with no tracking or external data transmission.
