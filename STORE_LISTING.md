Sim Companies Helper

Short Description:
Level up your Sim Companies empire! Real-time profit calculators, market comparisons, and chat tools to help you run a more efficient business.

Long Description:
Hey CEO! Ready to optimize your empire?

If you love Sim Companies but hate constantly switching tabs to check market prices or wrestling with spreadsheets just to handle your retail stores, this extension is for you.

Sim Companies Helper is a lightweight, friendly assistant that lives right inside your game dashboard. It adds the "missing features" that power users dream of, helping you make smarter business decisions in seconds.

What can it do right now?

Retail Profit Mastery:
- Profit Per Minute: Instantly see your PPM for any retail item. We color-code it (Green is good!) so you know at a glance if you are wasting a slot.
- Retail vs. Market: Wondering if you should retail those Aerospace inputs or just dump them on the Exchange? We calculate the difference for you, including transport costs!

Smart Production:
- Real-time Costing: See exactly how much it costs to produce an item based on accurate input costs.
- Custom Contracts: Easily calculate profits for custom contract prices without breaking out the calculator.

Recipe Extractor:
- On supported recipe pages, collect recipe data into a ready-to-copy JSON payload for external planning or sharing.

Upgrade Helper:
- Build copyable upgrade buy messages with discount and multiplier controls directly inside upgrade dialogs.

Chat Search (Beta):
- Stop scrolling endlessly! Search the Sales Chat for specific products. Find who is buying or selling right now.

Cashflow Insights:
- Get a quick breakdown of your daily income vs. expenses compared to yesterday.

What's New:
- After updates, see a quick in-game summary of release highlights with a link to the full release notes.

Safe & Secure
- Privacy First: We do not track you. Your data stays in your browser.
- Lightweight: No heavy background processes slowing down your game.
- Open Source: Built by a player, for players.

More Coming Soon!
We are actively building new features (better inventory management, specialized calculators, and more). This is just the beginning!

Note: This is a fan-made extension and is not officially affiliated with Sim Companies. But we love the game as much as you do!
